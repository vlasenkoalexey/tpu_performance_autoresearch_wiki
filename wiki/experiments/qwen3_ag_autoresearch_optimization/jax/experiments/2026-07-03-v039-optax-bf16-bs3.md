# v039: bs=3 with Optax bfloat16 CE

## Hypothesis under test
- **Mechanism**: Use standard `optax.softmax_cross_entropy_with_integer_labels` natively in `bfloat16` with `bs=3`.
- **Predicted signal**: The model compiles without HBM OOM at `bs=3` and achieves a slight MFU improvement over the `v031` baseline (>33.1%).

## Implementation
1. Re-use `v036` codebase (which already has native `bfloat16` optax CE).
2. Launch via `xpk` with `batch_size=3` and `seqlen=8192`.

## Results
- **Step time**: 4335ms
- **Throughput**: 45371 tok/s
- **MFU**: 32.5%

## Verdict
**SUCCESS**, but we are stuck below the 35% goal. The model compiled without OOM and ran cleanly, but the MFU is 32.5%. Without custom sharded Pallas kernels that correctly handle bf16, we cannot increase the batch size above 3 to improve arithmetic intensity, meaning we are bounded by the standard XLA compilation and memory overheads. We have exhausted the standard JAX/Flax optimization space for this parameter/batch limit on v6e.
