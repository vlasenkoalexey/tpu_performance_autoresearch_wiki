# v059-tp2-bs4

## Hypothesis under test

**Hypothesis**: Fixing the sharding axes (reversing fsdp/tp) and applying nnx.remat properly to the scanned Qwen3 layers will stop XLA from unrolling the model and executing massive cross-device gathers, thereby preventing the 53 GiB compilation OOM and allowing TP=2 bs=4 to run successfully.

**Mechanism**: We fixed the 2D sharding specification for TP=2 in `sharding.py`, applying TP to the output channel dimension for column-parallel layers (q/k/v/up/gate) and to the input channel dimension for row-parallel layers (o/down). We also replaced `jax.checkpoint` with `nnx.remat` in `modeling_qwen3.py` to prevent XLA from unrolling `Qwen3ScannedLayers`.

**Predicted signal**: Expect standard Optax CE to successfully compile and run without OOM at bs=4. The profile should show standard TP collective overheads (all-reduce), and MFU should increase due to better arithmetic intensity.

**Falsification criterion**: step MFU at bs=4 with TP=2 does not exceed the current frontier (32.5% MFU) over the median of steps 10-50, or TP=2 still fails to compile due to OOM.
