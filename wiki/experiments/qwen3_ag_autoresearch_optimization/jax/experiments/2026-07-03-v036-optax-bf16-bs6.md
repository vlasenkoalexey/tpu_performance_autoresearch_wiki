## Verdict
**FAILED**. The model OOMed during XLA compilation, using 42.74G out of 31.25G HBM (exceeded by 11.50G). Although removing the `.astype(jnp.float32)` on the logits avoids upcasting the dense output tensor, `optax.softmax_cross_entropy_with_integer_labels` performs a massive reduction across the vocabulary dimension internally. Without a custom sharded/fused cross-entropy kernel or explicit chunking, XLA creates temporary unsharded buffers or replicates computation, blowing up the memory. `bs=6` is still too large for standard optax CE on v6e.
