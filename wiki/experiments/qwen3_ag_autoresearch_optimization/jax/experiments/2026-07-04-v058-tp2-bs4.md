# v058-tp2-bs4

## Hypothesis under test

**Hypothesis**: Sharding the model and activations with Tensor Parallelism (TP=2) will free enough per-chip HBM to unblock batch scaling to bs=4 or bs=6 without OOMing during the cross-entropy backward pass.

**Mechanism**: We will apply TP=2 sharding across the DCN or ICI, ensuring activations and parameters are sharded across 2 chips. This should relieve HBM pressure and allow standard optax CE to run at bs=4 without hitting the 31.25 GB HBM limit.

**Predicted signal**: Expect standard Optax CE to successfully compile and run without OOM at bs=4. The profile should show TP collective overheads (all-reduce), but MFU should increase due to better arithmetic intensity.

**Falsification criterion**: step MFU at bs=4 or bs=6 with TP=2 does not exceed the current frontier (32.5% MFU) over the median of steps 10-50, or TP=2 still fails to compile due to OOM.
