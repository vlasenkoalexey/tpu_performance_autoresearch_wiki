# v037: bs=4 with Optax bfloat16 CE

## Hypothesis under test
- **Mechanism**: Use standard `optax.softmax_cross_entropy_with_integer_labels` natively in `bfloat16` with `bs=4`.
- **Predicted signal**: The model compiles without HBM OOM and runs faster than `bs=3`, achieving MFU > 35%.

## Implementation
1. Re-use `v036` codebase.
2. Launch via `xpk` with `batch_size=4` and `seqlen=8192`.

## Results
- **Step time**: N/A
- **Throughput**: N/A
- **MFU**: N/A

## Verdict
**FAILED**. The model OOMed during XLA compilation at `bs=4`, using 34.03G out of 31.25G HBM (exceeded by 2.79G). Even in pure `bfloat16`, the standard `optax.softmax_cross_entropy_with_integer_labels` expands massively during the vocabulary reduction at `bs=4`. The program space consumed 28.31G. Without custom chunking or a fused kernel, the intermediate buffers generated by XLA during standard Cross-Entropy compilation exceed the strict v6e constraints even at `bs=4`.
