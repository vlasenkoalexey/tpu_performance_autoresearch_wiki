# v023: TP=1 FSDP=8 with bs=2 (global 16) and fused projections

## Hypothesis under test
- **Mechanism**: We discovered that `v021` and `v022` actually ran with `TP=1, FSDP=8` because `--tp_parallelism` was not passed properly to `fire`. But they used `batch_size=1` (global_batch=8) which yielded 26.9% MFU, a regression from `v020` (32.3%) due to the memory/compute imbalance of the huge fused matrices. Instead of fighting TP=2 communication overheads or padding inefficiencies, we just double the `batch_size` to 2, increasing the global batch to 16.
- **Predicted signal**: The memory of `bs=2` at `seqlen=8k` with FSDP=8 and gradient checkpointing should fit in 31G HBM. The increased compute intensity over the same parameter-gathering communication volume will push the MFU beyond 35%.
