# v025: Unfused Layers, Vocab Parallel CE, bs=16

## Hypothesis under test
- **Mechanism**: Fused layers break FSDP compute-communication overlap by increasing the granularity of the `AllGather` operations. Chunked cross-entropy destroys memory bandwidth by reading the 1.2 GB `lm_head.weight` 8 times per step. By reverting to the unfused layers from `v020`, replacing chunked cross-entropy with a Vocab Parallel Cross Entropy, and doubling `batch_size` to 2 (global 16), we eliminate both bottlenecks and double compute intensity.
- **Predicted signal**: MFU should break past the 32.3% baseline and exceed 35%.

## Implementation
1. Re-use `.repo/2026-06-27-v020-splash-attn-only` which had unfused layers and 32.3% MFU.
2. Update `model/sharding.py` to shard `lm_head.weight` as `("fsdp", "tp")`.
3. Update `train.py` `_ce` to use `jax.nn.one_hot` to ensure GSPMD leverages the Vocab Parallelism without materializing the full vocab tensor.
4. Launch with `batch_size=2` (global_batch=16).
